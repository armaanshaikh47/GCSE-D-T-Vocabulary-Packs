📌 ESL Strategy — Vocabulary Notebook: Translation is hidden on purpose. Read the English definition first and try to picture it. Only press 中文 / Русский if you are stuck. Then, in your notebook, write the word, the English definition in your own words, and one original sentence about a real product. Copying the definition does not build memory — rewriting it does.

Retrieval Quizzes

Do these without looking at the vocabulary tab. Getting it wrong first, then checking, builds stronger memory than reading the answer. Your best score for each quiz is saved.

📌 ESL Strategy — Productive Failure: Guess before you check. A wrong guess that you then correct is remembered about twice as well as an answer you simply read. Your score here is not a grade — it is a map of what to revise.

Quiz 4 — Say it in English L1 → English recall

You know the concept in your own language. Now produce the English word the examiner needs to see. This is the only place translation is used — as a prompt, never as an answer.

Word Drill

Ten words at a time, chosen at random. Read the meaning, write the word. Every word in this pack will come up eventually — the four quizzes above only reach some of them. A word counts as learned once you have recalled it correctly here.
